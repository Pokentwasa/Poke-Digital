Website Concept

Ground Culture

A proposed digital direction for Ground Culture, bringing its food, culture, events, community and locations into one cohesive online experience.

groundculture.co.za

The concept walkthrough is being finalised and will appear here shortly.

The Direction

The concept focuses on presenting Ground Culture as more than a restaurant. The experience brings together food, comedy, events, community, locations and hire opportunities while giving each part of the brand a clearer place within the customer journey.

  1. 01

    Brand Story

    A clearer introduction to what Ground Culture represents beyond food alone.

  2. 02

    Events & Entertainment

    Greater prominence for comedy, live music, sport and recurring experiences.

  3. 03

    Food

    A stronger visual presentation of the menu, food trucks and locally rooted offering.

  4. 04

    Locations

    A clearer way to discover the different Ground Culture locations and what each offers.

  5. 05

    Hire

    Dedicated journeys for venue hire, food truck hire, private events and corporate opportunities.

  6. 06

    Customer Journey

    Simpler pathways around visiting, eating, attending, ordering and enquiring.

Built Around the Brand

Culture before convention.

Movement without distraction.

Strong storytelling. Clear actions.

Built to feel like Ground Culture, not a template.
