FAQs

How much does brand design cost in Cape Town?

It depends on what's needed — a logo on its own is a different job to a full identity with print and social templates. We scope each project after a discovery call and confirm pricing in writing before design begins.

What do I receive at the end of a brand design project?

The final logo files, your colour and typography system, and the applications scoped for the project — for example stationery, print pieces or social templates — in formats ready for print and screen.

Can you design the brand and build the website?

Yes. Brand and web design happen under one roof, so the identity is designed with the website in mind and carried across consistently.

Do you work with businesses outside Cape Town?

Yes. We're based in Cape Town and work digitally, collaborating with clients across South Africa and beyond.
