FAQs

How do I know if my website needs a redesign?

Common signs: the site looks dated next to competitors, it's slow or breaks on mobile, the brand has moved on but the website hasn't, or it simply isn't generating enquiries. If you're unsure, we're happy to give an honest read on a short call.

Will a redesign hurt my Google rankings?

Not if it's planned properly. We map every existing URL to its new equivalent, set up 301 redirects, and preserve the content and structure that's already earning rankings — the goal is a technical and visual upgrade, not starting your SEO over from zero.

Can you redesign the site without changing our brand identity?

Yes. A redesign can refine the digital execution of an existing brand without touching the brand itself, or extend to a fuller brand refresh — the scope is set with you upfront, not assumed.
