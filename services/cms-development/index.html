CMS Development

CMS Development Built Around How You Actually Work.

A content system is only useful if your team will actually use it. Poke Digital builds CMS-backed websites around the specific content a business needs to manage — not a generic admin panel with fields that don't map to how the site is really run.

Start a Project → View Our Work

Content built around your business

A restaurant needs to update its menu without touching layout. A property studio needs to add a new development with its own gallery and pricing table. A studio needs to publish new work without a developer's help. We design reusable content types around the specific things a business needs to change often.

What we build content systems for

  • Menus and seasonal offerings
  • Photo and project galleries
  • Events and bookings content
  • Articles and journal-style content
  • Portfolio and case study projects
  • Products, where relevant to the business

Reusable content types

Rather than hard-coding every page, we structure content as reusable types — a "project", an "article", a "menu item" — so adding a new one doesn't mean building a new page from scratch. This keeps the site consistent as it grows and keeps future updates cheap.

A dashboard your team can actually use

The editing experience matters as much as the public-facing site. We keep content workflows simple and clearly labelled, so updates don't require a manual — or a call to us every time something needs to change.

Built on solid foundations

Whichever CMS approach fits the project — a headless setup or a more traditional admin panel — the front end is still hand-built for performance and semantic structure, following the same principles as our web development work.
